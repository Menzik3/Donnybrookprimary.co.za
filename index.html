About Us

Who We Are

Donnybrook Primary School is a public primary school serving the Donnybrook community and surrounding rural areas in KwaZulu-Natal. We provide quality teaching and learning in a safe, caring and disciplined environment, where every child is natured to grow in knowledge, character and confidence.

Our History

For many years the school has served generations of families by providing quality education and promoting lifelong learning. The school continues to build on its proud tradition of excellence, discipline and community involvement.

Vision

To be an innovative Hub for quality teaching and learning that produces learners developed to exploit opportunities for a lifelong success.

Mission

To Facilitate quality teaching and learning in a conducive classroom environment everyday.

Core Values

  • Excellence
  • Openess & Transparency
  • Integrity

  • Empathy & Ubuntu
  • Professionalism

Academics

Curriculum

The school follows the South African CAPS curriculum, covering the following learning areas:

  • IsiZulu First Additional Language
  • English Home Language
  • Mathematics
  • Natural Sciences
  • Social Sciences
  • Life Skills
  • Creative Arts
  • Technology
Grade R – Grade 3

Foundation Phase

The earliest years of school, where learners build the literacy, numeracy and social foundations for everything that follows.

Grade 4 – Grade 7

Intermediate & Senior Phase

Learners deepen their knowledge across all subjects while developing independence, responsibility and leadership.

School Fees

Donnybrook Primary School is a Quintile 4 public school. School fees are determined annually by the School Governing Body. Parents who qualify may apply for fee exemption in accordance with the South African Schools Act.
